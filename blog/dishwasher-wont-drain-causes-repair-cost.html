Why Your Dishwasher Isn't Draining

The drain pathway inside a dishwasher involves several components working together. Water flows from the pump through the drain valve, then through a hose that connects to your home's plumbing system. If any single part fails or gets blocked, water pools at the bottom.

The most common culprit is a clogged filter basket. Over weeks of use, food particles, grease, and mineral deposits build up and trap water. You can often spot debris by lifting the filter yourself—it sits at the base of the tub and pulls straight out. If it looks dark or smells off, that's your first red flag.

Drain Hose Kinks and Clogs

Your dishwasher's drain hose runs from the appliance to either your sink's garbage disposal or directly into your home's drain line. If that hose gets kinked, pinched, or develops an internal blockage, water can't escape.

This happens more often than you'd expect, especially if the dishwasher was installed in a tight space or if cabinets were rearranged. A kink behind the unit might go unnoticed for months until drainage slows to a halt. We've also found that ice accumulation during Utah winters can partially freeze water inside hoses in unheated crawl spaces, restricting flow. Straightening the hose or replacing it costs between $150 and $300, including labor.

Pump Failure and Drain Valve Issues

If the filter is clean and the hose is straight, the pump itself may have failed. The pump is an electric motor that forces water out of the tub. When it burns out, water has nowhere to go.

Drain valves can also get stuck or develop cracks. Both issues require replacement, not repair. A new pump or valve typically runs $200 to $400 in parts alone, plus $100 to $200 in labor. We've replaced pumps in everything from budget models to high-end brands with equal success.

Installation Mistakes and Improper Setup

Occasionally, the problem isn't wear—it's how the dishwasher was hooked up in the first place. If the drain hose doesn't loop high enough under your counter before dropping to the sink, a siphon effect can form, pulling water back into the tub.

Similarly, if the hose was installed with an excessively low point or if it wasn't secured properly to prevent movement, gravity and vibration can cause blockages. We've seen this most often after a move or when homeowners relocate their dishwasher themselves. Correcting installation typically takes under an hour and costs $100 to $200.

West Jordan-Specific Factors That Impact Drainage

West Jordan sits on a hillside, and depending on which neighborhood you live in, your home's plumbing slope can vary significantly. Homes closer to Grandstaff Park or in the Redwood neighborhood tend to sit on steeper lots, which usually means better natural drain flow. But homes on flatter terrain near Old Bingham Highway or toward the Oquirrh Mountains may have shallow drain slopes, making them more prone to clogs.

Utah's water hardness is another factor. West Jordan's municipal water supply contains higher mineral content than many states. Over time, minerals like calcium and magnesium accumulate inside drain hoses and valves, reducing flow. If you have hard water, running a dishwasher with a rinse aid and cleaning the filter every two weeks can extend the life of your drainage system by months.

DIY Checks You Can Do Right Now

Before calling a technician, try these steps. First, stop the cycle and remove the bottom rack and filter basket. Use a flashlight to look for visible debris, hair, or food particles. If the tub is wet, scoop out standing water with a cup or towel.

Next, inspect the drain hose where it exits the dishwasher cabinet. Get down and look at the full length of the hose if you can access it. Straighten any obvious kinks by hand. Finally, check that your garbage disposal is working if your hose connects there—sometimes the disposal drain gets clogged, which blocks the dishwasher's water.

If the filter is visibly blocked, rinse it thoroughly under your kitchen sink with hot water. Most clogs dislodge easily. If water still pools after these checks, you likely need professional service.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a dishwasher drain repair take?

Most drain issues are resolved in 30 minutes to an hour. Pump or valve replacements may take up to two hours. We complete the majority of calls on the same day you schedule.

Is it safe to run a dishwasher with standing water inside?

No. Standing water can damage the pump and motor, and it creates a sanitation issue. Stop using the dishwasher until the drain problem is fixed.

Can I prevent dishwasher drainage problems?

Yes. Rinse dishes thoroughly before loading, run the garbage disposal before starting a cycle, and clean the filter monthly. Using rinse aid helps reduce mineral buildup in Utah's hard water.

Will my warranty cover a drainage repair?

It depends on the cause and your warranty terms. Manufacturer defects may be covered, but clogs from normal use typically aren't. Eagle Appliance Repair can review your documentation and let you know what to expect.
