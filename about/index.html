Our Story

About Eagle Appliance Repair

Eagle Appliance Repair has been serving West Jordan and the greater Salt Lake Valley since 2008, building a reputation for reliable, fast appliance repair across residential and commercial properties throughout the Wasatch Front. Founded by Yaron Hamer, our company grew from a single technician responding to emergency calls to a full-service team of certified repair specialists handling thousands of service calls annually. We understand the unique challenges homeowners face in Utah's climate, from hard water damage on refrigerators and dishwashers to dryer lint buildup accelerated by our dry winters. Our commitment is simple: get your appliances working again quickly so your family can get back to normal life.

Our team consists of EPA-certified technicians trained on all major appliance brands including Whirlpool, LG, Samsung, GE, Frigidaire, Maytag, and Bosch, with ongoing training to stay current with the latest smart appliance technology. We stock over 500 OEM parts in our service vehicles and maintain relationships with parts suppliers across Salt Lake County to minimize wait times and same-day repair rates. What sets Eagle Appliance Repair apart is our commitment to transparency: we provide upfront pricing with no hidden fees, explain repair options in plain language, and offer 12-month warranties on all parts and labor. We're not just fixing appliances; we're solving problems for our neighbors in West Jordan, South Jordan, Sandy, Murray, and 25+ surrounding communities.

Quality isn't negotiable at Eagle Appliance Repair. Every technician carries full credentials and carries $2 million in liability insurance to protect your home. We measure success not just by repairs completed, but by customer satisfaction scores consistently above 98% and repeat business from families who trust us year after year.

Meet the Owner

Meet Yaron Hamer, Owner

Yaron Hamer started Eagle Appliance Repair after spending over a decade working as an appliance technician for larger service companies, watching customers get overcharged and underserved. He saw an opportunity to build a business rooted in integrity, expertise, and genuine care for the communities he serves throughout the Salt Lake Valley. In 2008, Yaron invested his savings into Eagle Appliance Repair, beginning with his own truck and tools, answering every call personally and building relationships one family at a time across West Jordan's neighborhoods from Oakpointe to Oquirrh Hills.

Today, Yaron remains hands-on, personally training every technician and taking calls from customers who specifically request his expertise on complex repairs. He's deeply invested in West Jordan's community, sponsoring youth sports programs and serving local families through faith-based initiatives. Yaron's philosophy is straightforward: treat every customer's appliance emergency with the same urgency and care he'd want for his own family.
