Ice Maker Repair Services in West Jordan, UT

Ice maker failure is a common problem in West Jordan homes, especially in newer subdivisions near Daybreak and along Redwood Road where water quality and pressure fluctuate seasonally. When your refrigerator stops producing ice-whether it's a GE, Whirlpool, LG, or Samsung model-the culprit is usually a frozen water line, faulty inlet valve, or burnt-out motor module. Eagle Appliance Repair diagnoses the exact cause within 24 hours and sources replacement parts locally so repairs stay affordable and quick.

Our ice maker repair process begins with a visual inspection of the ice maker assembly, water line routing, and electrical connections. We test water pressure (should read 20 PSI minimum), check the inlet valve solenoid for continuity, and examine the evaporator coils for frost buildup. Once identified, we thaw frozen lines using approved methods, replace faulty components with manufacturer parts, and run a 10-minute production cycle to confirm function before we leave your West Jordan, Midvale, or Taylorsville home.

Signs You Need Ice Maker Repair

  • Ice maker produces little to no ice cubes despite running continuously
  • Water pooling or leaking from ice maker compartment onto freezer shelves
  • Ice cubes are small, misshapen, or hollow instead of solid
  • Audible clicking or humming from ice maker motor without ice ejection
  • Water line connecting to refrigerator is visibly frozen or cracked
  • Error codes on display panel or ice maker not cycling on and off
  • Frost or ice buildup covering the evaporator coils inside freezer
  • Water backup or slow draining from refrigerator defrost system
