Signs You Need Oven and Range Repair

  • Oven heats unevenly or fails to reach set temperature
  • Electric burners produce weak heat or won't heat at all
  • Gas burners ignite slowly or produce yellow/orange flames instead of blue
  • Range display panel shows error codes or won't illuminate
  • Oven door won't close properly or seal is cracked
  • Igniter clicks repeatedly but burners won't light
  • Convection fan runs constantly or makes grinding noises
  • Range produces burning smells or sparks inside the cavity

Oven and Range Repair FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does oven and range repair typically cost in West Jordan?

Most repairs range from $250-$800 depending on the component that failed. Simple igniter replacements cost $300-$400, while control board replacements run $500-$800. We provide flat-rate quotes before starting work so there are no surprises.

How quickly can Eagle Appliance Repair fix my oven or range?

We respond within 24 hours of your call and complete most repairs within 1-3 business days. If parts need ordering, we notify you of the timeline upfront. Emergency service is available for gas safety concerns.

What brands do you repair?

We repair all major brands including GE, Whirlpool, LG, Samsung, Viking, Electrolux, Maytag, Kitchenaid, and Wolf. We stock OEM parts for these brands and maintain technical certifications for their repair requirements.

Do you provide warranty on oven and range repairs?

Yes, all parts and labor are covered by a 12-month warranty. If a repaired component fails within 12 months under normal use, we replace it at no charge.

What if my range needs multiple repairs?

If your range has multiple simultaneous failures, we provide separate quotes for each repair. For appliances over 12 years old with three or more problems, replacement may be more cost-effective, and we'll discuss both options transparently.

Do I need permits for oven or range repair in West Jordan?

No permits are required for repair work. If you choose to replace rather than repair, gas line modifications may require a West Jordan City building permit, but repair work is exempt.
