Signs You Need Laundry Maintenance Services

  • Clothes not drying completely within normal cycle times
  • Unusual grinding, squealing, or thumping noises during operation
  • Water pooling inside washer drum or around base unit
  • Burning smell or smoke during dryer operation
  • Washer not draining or spinning clothes dry
  • Detergent buildup or mold visible in rubber seals
  • Longer cycle times than previously required
  • Visible rust or corrosion on exterior or interior components

Laundry Maintenance Services FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does laundry maintenance service cost in West Jordan?

Standard preventative maintenance runs $165-225 depending on appliance type and complexity. Washer inspections average $175, dryer vent cleaning is $150-200, and combo units run $225-275. Additional component replacement (belts, seals, hoses) costs $50-150 per part plus labor.

How long does a laundry maintenance appointment take?

Most appointments complete in 60-90 minutes from arrival to testing. Initial inspection typically takes 30-45 minutes, maintenance work follows, and we perform a full test cycle before departing. We schedule 2-hour appointment windows to ensure no rushing.

How often should I schedule laundry maintenance in West Jordan?

We recommend annual preventative maintenance for households doing 5+ loads weekly, or every 18 months for lighter use. High-mineral-content water areas like parts of West Jordan and Midvale may benefit from twice-yearly drain system maintenance.

What's included in your cleanup after maintenance?

We remove all debris, lint, and worn components from your home, vacuum around appliance bases, and dispose of materials responsibly. Your laundry room is left clean and organized exactly as we found it.

What warranty covers laundry maintenance services?

All Eagle Appliance Repair maintenance services include 12-month labor warranty covering any related issues from work performed. Parts carry manufacturer warranty from 1-3 years depending on component type and brand.

Do I need permits for laundry maintenance services?

Routine maintenance requires no permits in West Jordan, South Jordan, or surrounding municipalities. Vent line replacement or relocation may require inspection if it involves structural changes-our technicians advise on code compliance during service.
