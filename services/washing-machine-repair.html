Signs You Need Washing Machine Repair

  • Washing machine won't fill with water or fills very slowly during cold/warm/hot cycles
  • Drum doesn't spin or rotates weakly after rinse cycle completes
  • Water leaks underneath unit or from door seals during operation
  • Unusual grinding, squealing, or loud banging noises during spin cycles
  • Error codes displayed on digital control panel (E4, E5, OE, UE codes)
  • Clothes remain soaking wet after complete wash and spin cycles
  • Unit won't drain and water pools at bottom of tub
  • Burning smell or visible smoke coming from motor housing or control board
