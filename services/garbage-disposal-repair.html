Signs You Need Garbage Disposal Repair

  • Water leaking from under the sink onto cabinets or flooring below the disposal unit
  • Garbage disposal making grinding, squealing, or loud rattling noises during operation
  • Disposal not turning on despite having electrical power at the outlet
  • Food waste backing up into the sink instead of being ground and drained
  • Foul odors coming from the disposal even after running with hot water and ice
  • Disposal spinning but not grinding food waste effectively
  • Tripped circuit breaker or blown fuse every time the disposal runs
  • Water pooling inside the garbage disposal chamber during or after use

Garbage Disposal Repair FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does garbage disposal repair cost in West Jordan?

Most repairs range from $180 to $450 depending on parts needed. Seal and gasket replacement runs $200-$300, while impeller replacement costs $250-$400. Motor replacement or full unit replacement ranges $450-$850. Call 801-252-6257 for a free diagnostic and written estimate.

How long does garbage disposal repair take?

Most repairs complete within 1-2 hours from arrival. Diagnosis takes 20-30 minutes, parts replacement takes 30-45 minutes, testing takes 15-20 minutes, and cleanup takes 10 minutes. Complex jobs involving pipe damage may extend to 3 hours.

What if my garbage disposal is still under warranty?

Check your disposal documentation for manufacturer coverage. Most warranties cover motor failure but not damage from foreign objects or improper use. We can file claims with InSinkErator and Waste King on your behalf, potentially reducing your repair costs significantly.

Do you clean up after garbage disposal repair?

Yes. We clean all work areas, remove debris, dispose of the old unit if replaced, and leave your kitchen as we found it. All water is wiped from under-sink cabinets, and floors are swept.

What warranty do you provide on garbage disposal repairs?

Eagle Appliance Repair provides a 3-year warranty on replaced seals, gaskets, impellers, and electrical components. Motor replacements include the manufacturer's 5-7 year warranty. Labor is guaranteed for 30 days if the same problem reoccurs.

Do I need a permit for garbage disposal repair or replacement?

Utah does not require permits for garbage disposal repair or replacement in West Jordan or surrounding areas. However, if drain line modifications exceed 10 feet or involve septic systems in rural areas like Copperton, consult your local health department.
